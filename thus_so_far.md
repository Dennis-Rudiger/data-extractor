**Data Dictionary (selected fields & uses)**

- Item Code — Inventory identifier. Used for ABC Analysis, Movement Analysis, Dead Stock, Fast Movers, Margin Analysis.
- Customer Code — Unique customer identifier. Used for repeat customer analysis, CLV, retention, segmentation.
- Invoice Date — Date invoice was issued. Used for trend analysis, monthly/weekly sales, seasonality, forecasting.

Missions & Metrics
- Sales mission: Generate profitable revenue. Key metrics: Revenue, Volume, Margin, Average Invoice, Repeat Customers, Sales Rep Performance, Discount Rate.
- Inventory mission: Ensure products are available. Key metrics: Turnover, Fill Rate, Dead Stock, Stock-outs, Fast Movers, MOQ Violations, Forecast Accuracy.

Key AI questions to guide analysis: Where did growth come from? Which products are slowing? Who are our best customers? Is revenue diversified? Who depends on whom? What changed?

**Chart Library (when to use)**

- Line / Time series: Sales Trend, seasonality.
- Pie Chart: Category comparison (not for time series or concentration).
- Heatmap: Finding patterns (weekday/hourly sales, emergency requisitions).
- Pareto: 80/20 analysis (customers, revenue, products).
- Waterfall: Profit changes, margin movement, revenue drivers.
- Radar: Department or quarter comparison.

**Case Studies (examples of good writing)**

**Predictive Analysis Guide**

Every report should ask: If this trend continues, what happens? Consider Inventory, Sales, Customers, Suppliers, Cash Flow, Market Share, Operations. These are scenario questions — not firm predictions.

**Writing Style Guide (short rules)**

- Keep it to one page where possible.
- Use simple, measured language. Avoid: "Clearly", "Obviously", "Excellent", "Outstanding".
- Prefer phrases like: "The data suggests", "An emerging trend", "This deserves attention", "It is reasonable to expect".
